Know Your Customer — Standard and Simplified Measures

Standard customer due diligence applies to every client. It covers identity
verification, an understanding of the purpose of the relationship, and an assessment
of the client's risk profile based on geography, occupation, and expected activity.

Simplified measures may be applied only where the risk is demonstrably low: a
long-standing domestic client with a stable, low-value account and no cross-border
activity. Simplified measures never remove the identity verification requirement;
they reduce only the frequency of review.

Where documents are inconsistent — for example, the name on a passport differs in
spelling from the name on the application form — the inconsistency must be resolved
and recorded before the application proceeds. Unresolved inconsistencies are a
reason to escalate, not to approve.
