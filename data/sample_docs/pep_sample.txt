Politically Exposed Persons — Enhanced Due Diligence

A politically exposed person is an individual who holds, or has held within the last
five years, a prominent public office in Canada or abroad, including heads of state,
senior politicians, senior government officials, senior military officers, and senior
executives of state-owned enterprises. Family members and close associates of such
persons are treated the same way.

Enhanced due diligence is mandatory for every politically exposed person, regardless
of the assessed risk level. The measures include establishing the source of the
person's wealth and the source of the funds to be used in the account, and obtaining
senior management approval before the account is opened.

Where enhanced due diligence cannot be completed, the account must not be opened and
the matter must be escalated to the compliance officer.
